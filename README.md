1. locate to login.php

2. Sign in as a user:

    Example -

	a) Username: test1	password: testing123	[Sales Rep]
	b) Username: test2	password: testing321	[Admin]

3. Enjoy exploring:

	a) Sales Rep Users - able to see Dashboard, Customers (assigned to them), Record (of their customer), Leads (assigned to them), Calendar (personal notifications and interactions), Profile (personal)

	b) Admin Users - able to see Sales Team (all sales rep), Customers (all customers), Record (all records), Lead (all leads), Calendar (all notifications and interactions), Profile (personal)


* Sales Rep that a customer is assigned to can be different from their leads, as customers leads can be managed by different sales rep, whereas can be pulled by another sales rep initially
